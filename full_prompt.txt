Design choices: world coords stored as `x/z` only (game simulates in the XZ plane); `CITY_MESH.solid/visible` replaces the fragile mesh-name string heuristics (`street`, `building`, `collision`); `DELIVERY` makes the 100-pt rule queryable; `HIGH_SCORE` replaces `scoreboard.txt`.

# City Racer — What It Is

A **self-written 3D driving game in Java** (LWJGL/OpenGL, no game engine). You drive a van around a 3D city, pick up passengers at green markers, drop them at yellow, avoid traffic, and score before the 5-minute timer or 3 lives run out. Real `.glb` models (city map, Toyota van, BMW traffic, passenger) with box-geometry fallbacks, plus a HUD, scoreboard, and a teaching-focused OOP design.
